Media Buying

Paid campaigns managed in Meta Ads Manager

I plan, launch and optimize paid ad campaigns on Facebook and Instagram. The goal is simple: reach the right audience and turn views into real conversations with customers, while keeping the cost of every result as low as possible.

What I do

  • Build campaigns with different objectives: sales, engagement and messages.
  • Create several ad sets to test audiences, creatives and placements side by side.
  • Set daily and lifetime budgets, then move spend to what performs best.
  • Track cost per result, reach and impressions, and switch off what doesn't work.

Results snapshot (Oct 17 – Nov 15, 2026)

  • 10 ad sets managed in one account over 30 days.
  • Best ad set: 232 messaging conversations at about EGP 4.85 each.
  • Second best: 123 conversations at about EGP 6.67 each, with around 17K impressions per ad set.
